HOW TO PLAY

Move: Use WASD or arrow keys on desktop. On phones, drag on the arena or steer with the joystick.

Dash: Press Space or tap the action button to shove nearby rivals. Time it carefully; it has a cooldown.

Survive: Stay on the platform, grab power-ups, and avoid hazards as the arena shrinks. Last blob standing earns the most points.

Join friends: Create a room and share its invite link or six-character code. Rooms support 2–8 players.
